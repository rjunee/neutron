When it expires (or the bridge session itself dies at its ceiling), the bridge reports `trailerComplete=false`, and `forgeAgent` (line 1689) converts that report into a terminal throw — "the build wrapper was killed before it could report" — with NO check of whether the detached wrapper (launched via `nohup setsid`, deliberately outliving every bridge session) is still alive. The catch at line 6077 records it as `checkpoint: 'inner-error'`, terminal and unrecoverable, while the wrapper finishes into a worktree nobody will read.
