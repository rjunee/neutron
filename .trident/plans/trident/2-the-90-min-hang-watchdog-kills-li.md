# IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN — the hang watchdog must check run-scoped evidence before killing (Plan card #2)

Card: the 90-min hang watchdog killed 17 live builds in 30 days (incl. PRs #479/#480/#481/#484) because it reads only `last_advanced_at`, a column stamped on PHASE transitions that a long Forge round never re-stamps. Fix: declare a hang only on POSITIVE evidence — (1) live process for the run, (2) recent mtime on the run's own artifacts, (3) recent local ref movement on its branch — with three-valued observations (`activity`/`nothing`/`unknown`); `unknown` may never authorise a kill; the termination reason must disclose every probe and its answer; the reap must remain reachable (positive control). Do NOT raise the threshold, delete the watchdog, infer liveness from `last_advanced_at` alone, or let an unrunnable probe count as quiet.
