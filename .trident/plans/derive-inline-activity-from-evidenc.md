Tiers 2–3 (branch commits, dirty worktree) are recorded non-goals (acceptance e). Restart semantics: the inspector buffer dies with the process, so after a crash evidence reads 0 and every stale flag reads NOT active — acceptance (b) by construction. `GENERAL_WORK_BOARD_PROJECT_ID === 'general' === GENERAL_SCOPE`, so a `project_id` (or its General fallback) feeds `inspectorScopeKey` directly.
