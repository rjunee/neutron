## ARMED audit of the extracted Trident gates

### Scope and mechanical enumeration

I enumerated the subject with `rg --files trident/gates | sort`, removed the `*.test.ts` entries, and obtained 16 production-source files: `brief-integrity.ts`, `build-claim.ts`, `build-transition.ts`, `built-head.ts`, `escalation.ts`, `fix-lineage.ts`, `project-admission.ts`, `release-readiness.ts`, `result-contract.ts`, `review-artifact.ts`, `review-ci.ts`, `review-panel.ts`, `review-progress.ts`, `review-readiness.ts`, `review-suite.ts`, and `verdict.ts`. I then enumerated executable exports with anchored searches for `export function`, `export async function`, and exported constants. That produced 31 functions plus six data/configuration constants; the functions are accounted for below. The inline driver gates were enumerated separately with `rg -n -o 'G[0-9]{3}' trident/build-run.ts` and grouped only where they share the same reachability and source.

The decisive production-path finding is that the extracted driver stack has no repository production caller. `buildRun` is invoked by `createBuildHost` at `trident/build-host.ts:200`; `createBuildHost` is invoked by `createProjectBuildHost` at `trident/project-build-host.ts:81`; but the only non-test occurrence of `createProjectBuildHost` is its declaration at `trident/project-build-host.ts:58`. The source itself says a later launcher cutover owns invocation at `trident/project-build-host.ts:56-58`. Therefore a gate used only below that root is **NOT ARMED**, even when the local dependency plumbing beneath the unused root is internally complete.

Positive control for that absence: the same whole-tree, non-test search for `buildRun\(|createProjectBuildHost\(` finds the known call to `buildRun` at `trident/build-host.ts:200` and the declaration at `trident/build-run.ts:164`, while it finds no call to `createProjectBuildHost` beyond its declaration at `trident/project-build-host.ts:58`.

### Absence controls and classification conclusion

For the three newest sources I used one search over the production composition: `rg -n 'review(Readiness|Ci|Suite):|admission:' trident/project-build-host.ts`. Its positive control finds the known supplied `admission` dependency at `trident/project-build-host.ts:92`; it finds none of `reviewReadiness`, `reviewCi`, or `reviewSuite` in the complete `createBuildHost` object at `trident/project-build-host.ts:81-95`. They must be supplied by project policy/composition before their observations can exist. This confirms the expected parallel wiring finding; this report does not duplicate that implementation.

The existing outcome vocabulary is `GateResult`/`ReviewDecision` at `trident/build-run.ts:34-39`; the driver's `gateStop` maps `blocked` and `unknown` into terminal driver outcomes and lets `allow` continue at `trident/build-run.ts:167-174`. Missing review sources therefore default to `unknown`, not approval (`trident/gates/review-readiness.ts:71`, `trident/gates/review-ci.ts:19`, `trident/gates/review-suite.ts:32`).
