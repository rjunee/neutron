## Issue 1137 — post-review readiness waits instead of becoming unknown

### What changed

The post-review CI adapter now uses the same bounded readiness loop as the pre-review gate. `awaitSettledReviewReadiness` retains the settled `passed` or `failed` classification while retrying `pending` observations every 30000 ms within the 900000-ms host budget (`trident/gates/review-readiness.ts:72-113`). The project adapter calls it and preserves its terminal detail (`trident/project-observation-sources.ts:59-68`).

The driver passes its cancellation signal through both CI observations and handles `blocked` separately from `unknown` (`trident/build-run.ts:537-550`; `trident/build-host.ts:209-211`). The existing outcome vocabulary is `GateResult`: `blocked` refuses progress, `unknown` records unreadability, and `passed`/`failed` readiness becomes a known CI assessment. No new outcome or default case was added (`trident/gates/review-ci.ts:13-28`).

### Decisions

The existing readiness clock and budget remain the single continuous maintainer of settlement: the host loop re-observes independently of the review worker and its watchdog bounds a hung observer (`trident/gates/review-readiness.ts:75-108`). The helper was split so callers that need the final pass/fail classification can reuse the loop without creating a second retry implementation (`trident/gates/review-readiness.ts:111-113`).

The `blocked` classification was threaded through `ReviewCiSource` and `ReviewCiAssessment` instead of being folded into an unreadability message (`trident/gates/review-ci.ts:13-25`). This keeps “cannot determine” and “the answer is no” in separate branches through the driver (`trident/build-run.ts:538-550`).

The issue's cited line numbers were verified against `origin/main` and remained current: pending classifications were at `trident/gates/review-readiness.ts:37`, `:46`, `:50`, and `:52`; the collapse was at `trident/project-observation-sources.ts:63`; worker unknown terminates at `trident/build-run.ts:340`.

The complete production `reviewCi` call path was enumerated with `rg -n "reviewCi"` across the driver, host, project composition, adapter, and classifier: it is declared at `trident/build-run.ts:122`, called at `trident/build-run.ts:538` and `:548`, bound at `trident/build-host.ts:209-211`, supplied at `trident/project-build-host.ts:100`, and implemented at `trident/project-observation-sources.ts:59-68`.
