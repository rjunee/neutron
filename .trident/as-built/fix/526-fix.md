### Decisions

The clock is resolved per dispatch instead of at boot because the browser can persist the zone after composition; the maintenance mechanism is the live-turn read itself at `gateway/wiring/build-live-agent-turn.ts:1406-1415`, so warm sessions continuously refresh without restart. Missing or failed metadata is classified explicitly as unavailable at `gateway/wiring/build-live-agent-turn.ts:1413-1421` and `gateway/wiring/build-live-agent-turn.ts:851-853`; it never becomes the host timezone.

The USER.md repair is maintained by the authenticated owner-connect timezone hook at `open/wiring/app-ws.ts:1468-1478`, independently of the persona generator that failed to stamp older files. Its result vocabulary is `written | unchanged | missing | rejected` at `open/wiring/user-timezone-stamp.ts:12-15`; `written` invalidates the cache, `rejected` warns, and the default for `unchanged` or `missing` is no cache invalidation at `open/composer.ts:6020-6023`, because no file bytes changed.
