## Issue 1063 — stale-turn re-entry proof

### What changed

The reusable stale-entry fixture now accepts every option understood by `warmSession`, rather than only poison and exit state (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/__tests__/evict-deletes-only-its-own-entry.test.ts:179-184`). A new case gives the old warm session the requested tool surface while retaining its stale credential fingerprint, publishes a credential-fresh concurrent winner during the pooled-promise await, and asserts both that the turn returns that winner and that its promise remains the pool entry (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/__tests__/evict-deletes-only-its-own-entry.test.ts:308-325`).

No runtime lock was added. The continuously maintained invariant remains the pre-publish identity comparison: a turn whose original pool entry has been replaced re-enters the complete reuse decision procedure before it can spawn or publish (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/spawn.ts:1678-1719`). That mechanism is evaluated by the stale turn itself and does not depend on the concurrent winner continuing to run.

### Guarantee enumeration and decision

The historical change was enumerated from the complete `spawn.ts` diff of commit `8f477365`, not from its title. Its per-key lock covered one outcome, one replacement per pool key, across two suspension shapes: the pooled-session await and the additional async installed-server resolution. Current code awaits the pooled promise at `runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/spawn.ts:1493-1495`, resolves the installed-server configuration at `runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/spawn.ts:1563-1577`, identity-guards eviction of the original pool entry at `runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/spawn.ts:1649-1669`, and checks again immediately before publication at `runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/spawn.ts:1706-1722`.

Coverage of both historical suspension shapes is therefore explicit. The installed-server case launches two concurrent turns after a settings change and requires exactly one replacement, one pool entry, and one retirement (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/__tests__/owner-mcp-servers.test.ts:678-717`). The new credential-only case covers the pooled-promise await without relying on the installed-server resolver and requires the stale turn to reuse the already-published winner (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/__tests__/evict-deletes-only-its-own-entry.test.ts:308-325`). Cold calls remain deduplicated by the uninterrupted read-to-publish path, whose existing test requires one child from two concurrent starts (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/__tests__/owner-mcp-servers.test.ts:594-626`).

The bounded-contention outcome joins the existing `repl_unreconciled` error taxonomy and therefore skips credential cooldown by default (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/spawn.ts:1372-1388`). At the cap it is explicitly a retryable refusal, never an indeterminate observation or a spawn (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/spawn.ts:1708-1717`). Its existing test passes the thrown value through the production classifier and requires `repl_unreconciled` while asserting that no spawn occurred (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/__tests__/evict-deletes-only-its-own-entry.test.ts:501-531`).

The historical lock helpers are absent from `origin/main`: one `git grep` searched for `withGetOrSpawnLock|getOrSpawnSessionLocked|currentBeforePublish`; the first two names produced no hit, while `currentBeforePublish` at `runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/spawn.ts:1706-1707` was the positive control.
