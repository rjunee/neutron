## 2026-09-17 — Bound review fires its isolated workflow panel

### What changed

The ordinary project-build launcher remains the sole firer for build rows. `open/composer.ts:1188-1210` constructs it. The composer now constructs the retained dynamic-workflow panel firer at `open/composer.ts:1214-1217` and supplies it as `fire_review_panel` at `open/composer.ts:7193-7198`.

`gateway/composition/input/misc-input.ts:63-68` carries that panel seam into the composition, `gateway/composition/build-core-modules.ts:632-638` passes it to the orchestrator, and `trident/orchestrator.ts:184-191` / `trident/orchestrator.ts:1825-1838` deliver it to the bound-review path. `trident/bound-review.ts:148-158` gives only that firer to `executeBoundReview`'s isolated panel.

### Decision

The panel uses the retained dynamic workflow because `trident/review-run.ts:251-310` creates and polls a separate panel database, while `trident/project-launcher.ts:120-121` reserves only in its composition store. Routing the panel through the launcher therefore cannot reserve its row. The two firers serve distinct row ownership: one for durable project builds and one for an isolated review panel; there is no flag or fallback path.
