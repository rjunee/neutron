## Issue 1061 — moved-head resume coverage and fresh rebuild identities

### What changed

The offline driver harness now stops process 1 after its built checkpoint, advances the assigned branch with a real commit while the driver is down, and resumes through the real host. It proves that the moved revision is preserved but not silently adopted: process 2 dispatches plan and build before review, uses new round-one step identities, and merges both the intervening commit and the rebuilt output (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:993-1030`). The harness footer no longer lists moved-head resume as uncovered (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:1155-1158`).

The production defect exposed by that case was stale result identity reuse. Exact head equality is already the fast-path gate (`trident/build-run.ts:258-277`), but the rebuild fallback always called `planAndBuild(0)`. Step IDs include role and round (`trident/build-run.ts:308-315`), so a resumed rebuild could observe process 1's retained `plan:0` result instead of dispatching. The fallback now uses the checkpoint-derived `firstRound` on resume and retains round zero for a fresh build (`trident/build-run.ts:459-464`).

### Decisions and invariant

The host, not the worker being recovered from, continuously maintains the identity invariant: it derives every step ID from run, role, and round before saving the pending checkpoint (`trident/build-run.ts:308-316`). A resumed rebuild therefore advances to the persisted round and cannot depend on the earlier worker deleting or repairing its result. Fresh builds keep their established round-zero identity (`trident/build-run.ts:459-464`).

The behavior stays within the existing `BuildRunOutcome` vocabulary (`trident/build-run.ts:138-146`); stale trailer corroboration continues to use the existing `failed` / `built-head-unverified` classification (`trident/build-run.ts:380-390`).
