## Issue 1061 — moved-head resume coverage and fresh rebuild identities

### What changed

The offline driver harness now stops process 1 after its built checkpoint, advances the assigned branch with a real commit while the driver is down, and resumes through the real host. It proves that the moved revision is preserved but not silently adopted: process 2 dispatches plan and build before review, uses new round-one step identities, and merges both the intervening commit and the rebuilt output (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:993-1030`). The harness footer no longer lists moved-head resume as uncovered (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:1155-1158`).

The production defect exposed by that case was stale result identity reuse. Exact head equality is already the fast-path gate, but the rebuild fallback always called `planAndBuild(0)`. Step IDs include role and round, so a resumed rebuild could observe process 1's retained `plan:0` result instead of dispatching.

The fallback now advances to `firstRound` **only when the head moved** — recorded head a full OID, measured head a full OID, and they differ (`headMoved`). Every other rebuild keeps round zero. That narrowing was a correction in review: the first cut advanced on EVERY resume, and four existing `build-run` cases pin that an absent head, a wave task, and a ralph task rebuild reuse round zero (G029, G038, ralph step identities, wave resume) — all four went red. The lane ran the harness and not the adjacent unit surface.

### Decisions and invariant

The host, not the worker being recovered from, continuously maintains the identity invariant: it derives every step ID from run, role, and round before saving the pending checkpoint (`trident/build-run.ts:308-316`). A rebuild after a MOVED head therefore advances to the persisted round and cannot depend on the earlier worker deleting or repairing its result. Fresh builds, and resumes whose head did not move, keep their round-zero identity.

The behavior stays within the existing `BuildRunOutcome` vocabulary (`trident/build-run.ts:138-146`); stale trailer corroboration continues to use the existing `failed` / `built-head-unverified` classification (`trident/build-run.ts:380-390`).
