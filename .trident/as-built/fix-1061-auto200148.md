## Issue 1061 — offline panel-stop coverage

### What changed

The offline driver harness now drives two real panel outcomes that its own coverage inventory previously excluded. The fixture can emit an unresolved `COMMENT` through the ordinary verdict payload (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:213-225`) and can make the adversarial panel seat return the brief-sanctioned blocked envelope (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:243-267`). The latter travels through the real decoder and becomes the review source's existing `unavailable` observation (`trident/project-review-source.ts:113-120`).

The `COMMENT` case requires the exact unresolved-verdict refusal, the complete review/synthesis dispatch sequence, no fix, an open PR, and an unchanged base (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:887-901`). The unavailable-seat case requires the configured-seat refusal, no synthesis or fix, an open PR, and an unchanged base (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:903-918`). The harness inventory now leaves the still-uncovered deferred-seat and synthesis-disagreement cases explicit (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:1191-1197`).

### Existing vocabulary and decisions

No production outcome was added. The existing seat taxonomy is `completed | deferred | unavailable | rate-limited` (`trident/gates/review-panel.ts:21-30`). Its non-completed default is a named orchestrator block (`trident/gates/review-panel.ts:85-90`), while `COMMENT` belongs to the verdict taxonomy and becomes the existing unresolved-verdict block (`trident/gates/review-panel.ts:123`). The driver maps a blocked review decision to a terminal review-phase outcome addressed to the orchestrator (`trident/build-run.ts:138-141`, `trident/build-run.ts:203`).

The fake worker stayed literal. It still derives the output fields solely from the brief and omits `result` when blocked (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:258-267`). The new controls select an existing payload or blocked-envelope shape; they do not stub the panel gate or its source (`open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:182-185`, `open/__tests__/project-build-e2e.test.ts:501-505`). No product or specification decision changed.
