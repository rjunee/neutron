## 2026-09-16 — Let progressing wakeup turns outlive the inactivity window

### Change and evidence

The wakeup previously passed its four-minute value only to the reminder composer,
whose collector aborts at that fixed wall. The value is now an explicit REPL
inactivity window at `gateway/proactive/work-wakeup.ts:748`, where PTY activity
and the runtime's classified working observation can extend the turn. The
collector and REPL also receive the 45-minute absolute ceiling at
`gateway/proactive/work-wakeup.ts:749` and `gateway/proactive/work-wakeup.ts:761`.
The ceiling is nine five-minute sweep cadences: it allows substantial tool work
but bounds a noisy livelock (`gateway/proactive/work-wakeup.ts:122-127`).

The existing runtime contract defines `turn_timeout_ms` as inactivity reset by
PTY bytes and spared by a current working control (`runtime/substrate.ts:82-93`).
The persistent adapter measures each timeout decision from a fresh worker
observation (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/pool.ts:804-829`), spares a
working observation before the ceiling (`runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/pool.ts:841-845`),
and kills silence or the ceiling at `runtime/adapters/claude-code/persistent/pool.ts:873-883`.
That mechanism continuously maintains the invariant and does not depend on the
worker replying: the host-side watchdog performs the observation and decision.

Failures retain the total `failed` count and add `failed_no_progress` and
`failed_budget_ceiling` at `gateway/proactive/work-wakeup.ts:369-373`. The
existing `SubstrateCallError.code` vocabulary distinguishes the runtime's
`turn_timeout` from the collector's `aborted`; because both absolute timers share
one deadline, elapsed time classifies a substrate timeout at that wall as the
ceiling outcome (`gateway/proactive/work-wakeup.ts:768-783`). The registered
taxonomy defaults `turn_timeout` to retryable and `aborted` to nonretryable at
`runtime/errors.ts:119-122` and `runtime/errors.ts:136-139`. Each failure log now
names `failure_kind` and `dropped_work_ms` at
`gateway/proactive/work-wakeup.ts:784-791`, and the sweep summary publishes both
new counters at `gateway/proactive/work-wakeup.ts:875-880`.
